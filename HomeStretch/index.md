## Home Stretch

Home Stretch is a customizable interval timer for exercise routines, physical therapy homework, or anything else that needs a repeatable timer.

Features:
- Build your own timers: name each interval, set its length in seconds, and choose how many times the sequence repeats.
- Colour-coded progress ring with a large countdown, plus elapsed and remaining time for the whole session.
- A sound at every interval change, with a choice of four alarm tones.
- Automatically logs finished sessions with the date, repetitions completed, and duration.
- Export your activity log as a CSV file to share with a therapist or trainer.
- iPhone and iPad, portrait or landscape, light or dark.

Home Stretch is listed on the App Store as **Home Stretch Timer**.
